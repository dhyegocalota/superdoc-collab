## Edge cases found during execution

- Polling is one second and the fake streams a word every 400 ms, so the card shows bursts of two or three words once a second; each word inside a burst appears about 40 ms apart. Outcome: by design, not a defect.
- A topic click and a non-topic selection both showed the busy label for about 2 s, then about 50 ms with neither the busy label nor any word (the first partial reveals zero words), then the first word at about 2.1 s. Outcome: a one-frame blank card, cosmetic.
- When the first partial arrives while an older sentence is on the card, the old sentence is replaced by the growing partial. Outcome: by design.
- One held model call made 2 requests reach the fake model (the deadline retry) and the failure line showed at 30.2 s, not 60 s. Outcome: matches one retry started at the deadline; confirm that the hourly budget counts it once as the spec claims.
- Port 5000 on this machine is held by macOS Control Center, so the walk used 5050 with the fake model on 5099. Outcome: environment note.
