Ana signs in, New document, clicks the first page, End, types 120 characters at 20 ms, pastes 2000 characters; bruno joins through the share link; both type at 20 ms per key for 10 s. Steady state is fine, about 258 to 264 frames per second per page (six frames per key). In the second after typing stops, the per-page frame counter shows 1248 frames from Ana's page and 1282 from Bruno's in one second; the server logs `Collaboration socket closed for sending too many messages` twice (watch log `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-dhyegocalota-orca-workspaces-superdoc-take-home-final-visuals/3482769f-2fc2-4b3a-9594-26daaf8cc628/scratchpad/socket-walk2/watch.log`, count 2 at 13:29:27, 250 ms after typing ended) and both sockets close and reopen. Expected: no breach line, all 120 + 2000 + 436 + 436 characters in both editors. Observed: both editors converge to 2558 of 2992 characters, Ana's 436 concurrent characters are missing for both authors, Ana sees the banner "That edit could not be applied". Reproduced three times (attempts at 13:09, 13:21 and 13:29; per-second counters on the last). Not reproduced with Ana alone at 26 characters or at 120 characters. Cause is not established: the page flushes a large backlog in one second after typing ends. The cap of 1000 per second is therefore not a bound that real typing cannot reach on a long document with a second author; either the flush needs smoothing or the cap needs another look. Regression test requested from the developer: a unit or e2e case with a backlog flush of 1300 frames in one second that must either not close the socket or not lose the unsynced text.
