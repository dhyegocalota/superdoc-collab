## Edge cases found during execution

- A budget of one heavy room, ana holds A with an open tab, bruno opens B: 503 with "The server is holding as many large documents as it can; try again in a few minutes" and Retry-After 60, Try again and Back to documents shown (screenshots 09-bruno-refused-1280.png and 09-bruno-refused-375.png). Ana reloads A at the budget and it opens (claim 1). Bruno's blank opens at the budget. When ana's context closes, bruno's retry opens at once (0 s), not after a minute: the room unloads when its last socket leaves. Evidence: .pr-media/instant-open-2/walk/budget-walk.out.
- Ana's tab stays open and idle for 65 s at the budget: bruno's first poll is refused and the server logs "Closed the sockets of a heavy room nobody edited, to make room for another"; a second poll 3 s later is still 503 until the room unloads; the next retry opens. Ana's still-open tab keeps the editor visible, no banner, and a line she types afterwards is kept: a fresh ana tab sees it. Evidence: .pr-media/instant-open-2/walk/server-budget-stack.log, 11-ana-typed-after-eviction-1280.png.
- A budget of 4000 MB, ana holds two heavy rooms, the third heavy open is 503 with the same "holding as many large documents as it can" sentence (screenshot 12-ana-third-heavy-refused-1280.png). The sentence names the server, not the per-user cap of two, so ana is told to wait for something her own two open documents cause. A blank opens and the first room reloads (200). Bruno gets 404 for ana's document. Evidence: .pr-media/instant-open-2/walk/user-cap-walk.out.
- A third heavy upload by one owner while two of theirs are still converting is 429 (queue full), not the 503 busy sentence, so the dialog differs by which limit trips first. Evidence: first run of the user-cap walk, console 429 on POST /api/documents.
- With the ceiling at 60 s and one conversion of about 60 s running, a later user's heavy upload is already 503 once the learned rate matches the measured one; the first walk's second upload (estimate 40 s) was accepted. Evidence: small walk at the ceiling stack, 503 on bruno's second upload.
- Heavy document queued but not yet running, polled at the ceiling: the open route answers 200 queued three times (claim 2). Blank create via UI and the 2 MB brief DOCX upload both open while the heavy lane is over the ceiling (screenshot 08-small-opened-while-over-ceiling-1280.png).
- Release lines in the log carry resident bytes only, not heap bytes: before 2,561,687,552 and after 2,549,366,784 in the first room, before 4,124,672,000 and after 4,101,636,096 at the 4 GB peak, pauses 7 to 375 ms. Resident memory is returned only later (the process stood at 41 MB at the end). Evidence: .pr-media/instant-open-2/walk/server-ceiling-stack.log.
- The small warning icon beside the refusal sentence is a few pixels wide against 24 px of text at 1280 and 375 (09-bruno-refused-1280.png). Cosmetic.

## Coverage honesty

- The memory constants are extrapolations of the one measured document; the E2E scripts the limits low instead of filling a container.
- A real 8 GB container is not reproduced; the capped repro (server heap 3,276 MB) measured the heap at N rooms, see the spec.
- Two loads at the same instant are not budgeted twice: the load spike is counted once.
